TrialMitra : Your Guide to Clinical Trials in India.

Bridging the Gap Between Clinical Trials and Patients:
TrialMitra is a patient-centric digital platform designed to make clinical trial information more accessible, understandable, and easier to navigate. Our goal is to bridge the gap between clinical research and patients by simplifying complex trial information and helping individuals explore potential clinical trial opportunities.

About the Project:
Clinical trials play a crucial role in developing new treatments and advancing medical science. However, patients often face difficulties finding relevant clinical trials and understanding eligibility criteria, study requirements, and other essential information.
TrialMitra aims to address this challenge by presenting clinical trial information through a simple, user-friendly interface. By improving access to understandable information, we hope to empower patients to make more informed decisions and encourage greater awareness of clinical research.

Problem Statement:
- Clinical trial information can be difficult for patients to understand due to complex medical terminology.
- Finding trials relevant to a specific disease or health condition can be challenging.
- Patients may be unaware of available clinical research opportunities.
- The gap between clinical research information and patient awareness can limit participation in clinical trials.

Our Solution:
TrialMitra provides a centralized, patient-friendly interface for exploring clinical trial information. The platform is designed to simplify the discovery process and present important study details in an accessible format.
Our vision is to make clinical research more approachable, transparent, and accessible to the people it aims to serve.

Key Features:
- Patient-Friendly Interface: A simple and intuitive website designed with accessibility and ease of use in mind.
- Clinical Trial Discovery: Helps users explore clinical trial information relevant to different diseases and health conditions.
- Simplified Trial Information: Presents important study details in a more understandable format.
- Eligibility Awareness: Helps users understand the eligibility criteria specified for clinical studies.
- Improved Research Awareness: Encourages patients to learn about clinical trials and potential research opportunities.
- Responsive Design: Designed to provide a convenient browsing experience across supported devices.

Note: Features described here represent the intended platform capabilities. Availability depends on the current implementation.

Our Focus:
TrialMitra aims to make clinical trial information easier to explore across multiple disease areas, helping users discover relevant research without needing to navigate complex clinical research terminology independently.

Technology Stack:
The project uses web technologies to build and deploy its user interface.
- HTML5 — Website structure and content
- CSS3 — Styling and responsive layouts
- JavaScript — Interactive elements and user interactions
- GitHub — Source code management and collaboration
- GitHub Pages — Website hosting and deployment, if enabled for the repository

Future Scope:
Potential future enhancements include:
- Integration with publicly available clinical trial registries, such as the Clinical Trials Registry–India (CTRI).
- More advanced trial search and filtering options.
- Disease-specific trial discovery.
- Improved explanations of eligibility criteria and study procedures.
- Patient education resources about clinical research.
- Links to official trial records and verified study contacts.
- Additional accessibility and multilingual support.

Disclaimer:
TrialMitra is intended to support awareness and exploration of clinical trial information. It does not provide medical advice, diagnose medical conditions, or guarantee eligibility for any clinical trial.
Users should consult qualified healthcare professionals and the relevant clinical trial investigators before making decisions about participation. Trial information and eligibility should be verified against the official registry record.
